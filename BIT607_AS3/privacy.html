Privacy Statement

Snazzy Paws Grooming may collect the following information.

Information we collect

We collect personal information directly from you when you make an enquiry, book and appointment, contact us, or use our website, including your:

  • Name
  • Contact information
  • interactions with us
  • pet's information including its name, breed, age, health conditions

How we use your information

We collect your personal information to:

  • communicate with you to provide services, news, events or other relevant information
  • manage customer records
  • provide appropriate grooming services to your pet, maintain service records and ensure the wellbeing of your pet in our care
Providing some information is optional. If you choose not to enter your name and contact information, we will be unable to keep you up to date with your appointment or provide services to you. Website analytics Our website may use cookies to collect anonymous usage information to help us understand how visitors use our website and improve our website. The information collected is aggregated and does not identify individual visitors. This information may include: Pages visited Date and time of visits General usage patterns Device type Approximate location Pet photography consent We may take photographs of your pet during grooming. These photographs may be used on our website, social medical channels, and promotional materials. If you do not wish your pet’s photograph to be used for these purposes, please let us know at the time of booking or before publication. Sharing information We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties. Information may be shared only where required by law or when necessary to operate the website and its services. Retention of information We keep personal information only for as long as needed to provide our services, maintain business records, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. When information is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymised. Access and correction You have the right to ask for a copy of any personal information we hold about you and to ask for it to be corrected if you think it is wrong. If you’d like to ask for a copy of your information or to have it corrected, please contact us at admin@snazzypawsgrooming.co.nz.
